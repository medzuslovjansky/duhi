# Duh Slova — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

Duh Slova is a Discord bot that looks up words in the Interslavic dictionary. It is run on the official Interslavic Discord server (github.com/medzuslovjansky).

**What the bot receives.** When you use `/gledaj`, press one of its buttons or pick from its menu, Discord sends the bot that interaction: your user ID, the server and channel IDs, your Discord language and the text you typed. The bot does not read messages in channels.

**What the bot keeps.** Nothing. It answers and forgets. It has no database of users, servers or queries.

**Logs.** When an answer fails, the bot writes the interaction ID and the error to its server log. The log is deleted when the bot is next deployed.

**Public answers.** «Pokaži vsim» posts the answer to the channel; Discord shows who asked for it. Server moderators can delete such a message.

**Sharing.** Nothing is sold or passed on. The bot runs on a server rented from Hetzner Online GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany (EU); the log stays on that server. Discord itself processes your messages and interactions under its own Privacy Policy. The dictionary itself is public.

**Questions and deletion requests.** Open an issue at https://github.com/medzuslovjansky/duhi/issues.

**Changes.** We may update this policy. The current version is always at this address, with its date. If a change affects what data the bot collects or keeps, we announce it on the Interslavic Discord server before it takes effect.
